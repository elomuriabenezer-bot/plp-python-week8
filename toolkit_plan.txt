Tools

   Concepts: random numbers, input, while loop, conditionals, and f-strings.
2. To-Do List: The user can add tasks, view all tasks, and remove a task while the program is running.
   Concepts: list, append, remove, membership check, loops, conditionals, and f-strings.
3. Simple Calculator: The user enters two numbers and chooses addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division.
   Concepts: input, numeric conversion, conditionals, division-by-zero check, and f-strings.
